Our Story

Dependable Moving.
Local Roots.

Horsemen Moving Services is a locally owned and operated moving company serving Windsor-Essex County and the surrounding areas. We started with a straightforward goal: to provide the kind of moving service that we would want for ourselves.

Moving is one of the most stressful events in a person's life. We take that seriously. Our team approaches every job whether it's a small apartment or a full commercial relocation with the same level of care, professionalism, and attention to detail.

We believe in treating customers fairly, showing up when we say we will, and getting the job done right. No shortcuts, no excuses.
