What We Offer

Our Services

From a single apartment to a full commercial relocation, Horsemen Moving Services has you covered. Professional, careful, and dependable every move.

01 Residential

Residential Moving

Moving your family from one home to another is a big undertaking. We handle it with care from start to finish loading, transport, and delivery. Our team treats your belongings as if they were our own.

02 Apartment & Condo

Apartment & Condo Moving

Tight hallways, elevators, loading docks, and time windows we know apartment and condo moves require extra planning. We coordinate everything so the process runs smoothly and on schedule.

03 Office & Commercial

Office & Commercial Moving

Business moves need to be efficient and organized. We work with you to plan and execute the relocation with minimal disruption to your operations. Desks, filing systems, equipment, and more.

04 Local

Local Moving

Moving within Windsor-Essex County or to a nearby city? Our local moving service is fast, efficient, and priced fairly. No long-distance rates just professional service close to home.

05 Long-Distance

Long-Distance / Out-of-Town Moving

Relocating beyond the region? We provide out-of-town moving services with the same level of care and professionalism as our local moves. Contact us to discuss your destination and timeline.

06 Labour

Loading & Unloading

Already have a rental truck or POD? Hire our crew for loading, unloading, or both. We provide the muscle and expertise you bring the truck. A straightforward, cost-effective option.

07 Heavy Items

Furniture & Heavy-Item Moving

Sofas, dressers, appliances, safes, and other oversized items require special care. We have the equipment and techniques to move heavy and bulky pieces without damage to the item or your home.

08 Assembly

Furniture Disassembly & Reassembly

Bed frames, wardrobes, desks, shelving units we disassemble what needs to come apart and put it all back together at the new location. Hardware is kept organized and accounted for.

09 Labour Only

Moving Labour

For customers who already have a truck arranged, we offer moving labour services. Our experienced team handles the heavy lifting, packing, and placement so you don't have to.

10 Truck Rental

Truck Rentals

Need a truck as part of your move? Ask us about truck rental options available to complement your moving service. We can help coordinate the right size vehicle for your load.

11 Packing

Packing Services

Save time and reduce stress with professional packing. We use proper materials and techniques to protect your belongings during transit. Ask about packing supplies and full-service packing.
